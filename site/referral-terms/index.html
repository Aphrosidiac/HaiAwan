Share Awan, earn together.

Every Awan account has a link: awan.ffdev.studio/@yourname. When a friend signs up through it and subscribes, you earn 25% of their plan price on each eligible payment, and they get 25% off their first month of Pro or Max.

These are the standard rules. The Invite & Earn screen in the app shows the terms for your own link, including any special rate we've agreed with you.

What you earn

Commission is 25% of the plan's list price, before discounts and taxes. A friend on Pro at $20 a month earns you $5 per payment, even in their discounted first month. A friend on Max at $100 a month earns you $25 per payment.

You earn on eligible payments for 12 months from their first payment. They can cancel whenever they like. Twelve Max payments would earn you $300; that's an example, not a promise, because earnings depend on what's actually paid.

Yearly plans earn commission too. The friend discount applies to monthly plans.

How a referral counts

Your friend should open your link before they create their account. The link is remembered in their browser for up to 60 days, and once it's attached to their account it stays attached for future payments. Clearing site data or switching browsers can lose it.

An account can have one referrer, claimed before its first subscription. You can't refer yourself or set up referral loops between accounts. Disabled links can't create new referrals.

Getting paid

Each commission is held for 45 days after the payment it came from. Once your available balance reaches $25, email hello@ffdev.studio to arrange a payout. Smaller balances roll over. Payouts are made by hand, and the app shows your earnings, available balance and past payouts.

Full refunds and chargebacks reverse the related commission. If that happens after a payout, your balance can go negative until new earnings cover it.

During the private beta no real payments are taken, so no real commission is earned yet. Your link still works, and friends who join through it stay attached for when billing goes live.

Sharing and privacy

If you post your link publicly, say that you may earn from it. Your dashboard shows the friends you've invited by first name and last initial, with when they joined, whether they subscribe, and what you've earned from them. It never shows their email address. See the privacy policy for the rest.
